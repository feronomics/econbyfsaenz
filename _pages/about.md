I am a Guatemalan economics graduate and Senior Research Associate at the Abdul Latif Jameel Poverty Action Lab (J-PAL LAC), based in Guatemala City. My research interests lie in development economics, particularly human capital and nutrition.

I have experience supporting **randomized controlled trials (RCTs)**, including survey design, power calculations, fieldwork coordination, and data quality monitoring, alongside quantitative analysis using **Stata and R**.

My main project at J-PAL LAC, led by Karen Macours, studies constraints to scaling climate-resilient biofortified maize among smallholder populations in Guatemala. Alongside this assignment, I provide cross-project support on early childhood development, women's empowerment, and weather-triggered automatic cash transfers. Read more about my [research and project contributions]({{ '/research/' | relative_url }}).

Previously, I worked as a Research Assistant at ASIES Guatemala and a Data Analyst at Guatemala's National Institute of Statistics (INE). At Zona Trading, I developed an automated economic and financial dashboard with GARCH forecasts and authored five quarterly macroeconomic and U.S. equity-market reports.
