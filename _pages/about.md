I am a Guatemalan economist and Senior Research Associate at the Abdul Latif Jameel Poverty Action Lab, Latin America and the Caribbean (J-PAL LAC). My research interests lie in development economics, particularly at the intersection of human capital and nutrition.

My work combines causal inference and impact evaluation with the practical demands of field research: survey design, fieldwork coordination, data quality monitoring, and analysis in R and Stata. At J-PAL LAC, I support research on climate-resilient biofortified maize in Guatemala and contribute to projects on early childhood development and women's empowerment.

Previously, I worked as a Research Assistant at the Association of Research and Social Studies (ASIES Guatemala) and as a Data Analyst at Guatemala's National Institute of Statistics (INE). I hold a B.A. in Business Economics from Rafael Landívar University and have completed coursework in MIT's Data, Economics, and Design of Policy program.

## Research interests

- Development economics and poverty
- Human capital, education, and labor markets
- Nutrition and agricultural development
- Causal inference and impact evaluation
